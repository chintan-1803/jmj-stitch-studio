Meet Vaishali Dave

A Journey of Passion, Perseverance & Possibility

My stitching journey began in 2008, at just 16 years old , in Lunsar, Gujarat. What started as a simple interest became a lifelong passion, nurtured by my family’s encouragement and years of learning and dedication.

After building my boutique experience in India, moving to Adelaide meant beginning a new chapter. With the kindness of Aarti ben, the encouragement of Ankita ben and the support of my loved ones, I found the courage to continue doing what I love.

Starting again doesn’t mean starting from nothing. Every stitch carries the experience, gratitude and dreams that brought me here.

“Every stitch carries a little courage, a little love, and a dream that never stopped growing.”

Find answers to common questions about our stitching services, appointments, fittings and more.

What stitching and alteration services do you offer?

We offer custom stitching, designer blouses, Indian and Western outfits, children’s clothing, alterations and fitting adjustments. Contact us to discuss your specific requirements.

Do I need to book an appointment?

We recommend contacting us in advance to arrange a convenient time for discussing designs, taking measurements or collecting outfits.

Can you alter Western clothing?

Yes! We work with a variety of Western garments, including dresses, tops, trousers and other clothing. The alterations available depend on the garment and its construction.

Do you stitch Indian traditional outfits?

Yes. Our services include designer blouses, kurtis, salwar suits, lehengas and other Indian outfits, depending on the design and requirements.

How much do stitching and alterations cost?

Pricing depends on the garment, design complexity, fabric, fitting requirements and work involved. Please share your requirements for a personalised quote.

How long does it take to complete an order?

Completion time varies depending on the type of work, design complexity and current bookings. We’ll discuss an expected completion date when confirming your order.

Do you accept urgent stitching or alteration requests?

Urgent requests may be accommodated depending on availability and the work required. Additional charges may apply for priority orders.

Can I share a design or reference photograph?

Absolutely! You’re welcome to share photographs, sketches or design ideas. We’ll discuss what is achievable with your fabric and requirements.

Where is JMJ Stitch Studio located?

JMJ Stitch Studio is based in Northfield, Adelaide, South Australia. Please contact us to arrange an appointment and receive collection or visit details.

JMJ STITCH STUDIO · THE JOURNEY

Meet Vaishali Dave

Every stitch has a story.

Every stitch has a story, and mine is woven with dreams, determination, and the love of people who believed in me.

My journey with stitching began in 2008, when I was just 16 years old , in Lunsar, Gujarat. What started as a simple interest gradually grew into a passion that would become an important part of my life. I never imagined that something I began learning at such a young age would one day become the foundation of my own boutique.

Like every meaningful journey, mine was shaped not only by hard work and determination but also by the people who stood beside me.

My mother-in-law supported my passion by providing me with a sewing machine, while my mother showed her trust in my abilities by giving me her blouses to stitch. My Jethani became one of my first customers and encouraged others to try my work. These may have seemed like small gestures at the time, but for me, they were the beginning of something much bigger. Every little encouragement gave me another reason to believe in myself.

Over the years, I continued learning, practising, and improving my skills. Through hands-on experience, professional training with Hunar and Raja Rani Coaching , and more than seven years of running my own boutique in India, I developed a deep appreciation for the art of stitching. From traditional blouses and beautiful Indian outfits to custom creations and everyday alterations, every piece became an opportunity to learn something new.

Then life brought me to Adelaide, Australia, and with it came the challenge of beginning again.

I had years of experience, countless memories, and a passion I had nurtured for so long. But I was now in a new country, surrounded by unfamiliar faces and new challenges. Starting over wasn’t always easy, yet I believed that the skills I had built and the dreams I carried were worth continuing.

I realised that starting again doesn’t mean starting from nothing. It means carrying everything you’ve learned, every challenge you’ve overcome, and every little achievement with you into a new beginning.

In March 2026, one of my first friends in Adelaide, Aarti ben, gave me a small sewing machine that helped me restart my stitching journey. It was more than just a machine; it was a thoughtful gesture that reminded me how a little kindness can give someone the confidence to take their next step.

I am equally grateful to Ankita ben and all my family members who have supported, encouraged, and believed in me throughout this journey. Their love, trust, and encouragement have been an important part of every step forward.

Today, through JMJ Stitch Studio , I am slowly building something of my own here in Adelaide. Every new customer, every completed outfit, and every kind word of appreciation reminds me why I chose to continue doing what I love.

For me, stitching has never been just about fabric and thread. It is about understanding someone’s vision, paying attention to the smallest details, and creating something that makes a woman feel beautiful, comfortable, and confident.

JMJ Stitch Studio is more than a stitching studio to me. It represents years of learning, the trust of my customers, the support of my loved ones, and the courage to follow my passion even when life takes an unexpected turn.

I believe that dreams don’t have an expiry date, and a new beginning can be just as beautiful as the journey that brought you there.

Every stitch reminds me of where I started, how far I’ve come, and how much more there is to create.

JMJ Stitch Studio — By Woman, For Women.
